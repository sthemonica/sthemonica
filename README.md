# Hi, I'm Sthefanie 👋

**PhD student in Computer Science** at [Constructor University](https://constructor.university/) · Bremen, Germany 🇩🇪

I work in **computational social science**, using data-driven methods to analyze communication and understand **gender differences in tech**.
Before the PhD, I did an **MSc in Electrical Engineering** at UTFPR (Brazil) focused on AI and **explainable AI (XAI)**, and worked as a **Data Scientist** applying machine learning to real-world problems.

I love teaching and sharing knowledge — on my YouTube channel [@sthemonica](https://www.youtube.com/@sthemonica) and as **co-founder of [@canalifelse](https://www.youtube.com/@canalifelse)**. Feel free to reach out! 💬

### 🔬 Research interests
`Computational Social Science` · `Explainable AI (XAI)` · `Communication Analysis` · `Gender & Tech`
